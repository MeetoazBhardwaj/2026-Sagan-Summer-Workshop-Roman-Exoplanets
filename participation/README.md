# Participation

This directory is reserved for evidence of participation in the **2026 Sagan Summer Workshop: Exoplanets with Roman Surveys: Microlensing and Transits**.

Possible contents may include:

- selected screenshots from workshop sessions;
- an attendance letter or certificate, if issued and appropriate to publish; and
- a short participation note.

This section is intentionally secondary to the scientific and computational content of the repository. Its purpose is to provide context and provenance, not to turn the repository into a personal certificate archive.

Before publishing screenshots or documents, personally sensitive information and information about other attendees should be reviewed carefully.
