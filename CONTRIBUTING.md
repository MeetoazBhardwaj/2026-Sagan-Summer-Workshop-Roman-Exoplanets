# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through issues and pull requests.

## Important ownership and licensing terms

This is a public, collaborative, source-available project with non-commercial licensing for repository-original software and code.

Before any contributor-authored code, documentation, analysis, figures, or other copyrightable material is merged, the contributor must agree to the repository's `CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md` through a signing/acceptance process approved by the repository owner.

The purpose of that agreement is to allow the repository owner to maintain centralized ownership/control of accepted contributor-authored material, including the ability to enforce the license and maintain the project consistently.

Do not submit material that you do not have the legal right to contribute.

## Third-party workshop material

Do not alter authorship notices, copyright notices, citations, or provenance information in workshop-provided files.

The repository owner's license does not replace the rights or terms that apply to third-party workshop material.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a focused branch for your proposed change.
3. Make the smallest reasonable change needed.
4. Preserve the distinction between original workshop templates and completed participant versions.
5. Do not modify workshop-authored cells in completed notebooks unless the exercise itself explicitly requires that cell to be completed or changed.
6. Test notebook/code changes where practical.
7. Open a pull request describing what you changed and why.
8. Complete the contributor agreement process before the contribution is merged.

## Notebook policy

The repository keeps two separate forms of workshop exercises when redistribution rights permit:

- `workshop-materials/`: unchanged/default workshop files for learners who want to attempt the exercises themselves;
- `completed-notebooks/`: separate participant-completed copies with answers added only in designated exercise locations and outputs retained where practical.

Please do not overwrite the default copy with a completed version.

## Commercial use

Submitting a contribution does not grant contributors or downstream users a commercial-use license to repository-original material. Commercial use remains subject to the applicable license and any separate written permission from the copyright holder.
