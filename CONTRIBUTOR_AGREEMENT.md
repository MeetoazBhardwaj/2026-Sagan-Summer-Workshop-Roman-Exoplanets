# Contributor Agreement Requirement

This project is maintained under an owner-controlled contribution model.

## Purpose

The repository owner intends to retain centralized ownership and control over repository-original material while allowing public collaboration.

For that reason, **no third-party contribution containing copyrightable material will be merged until the contributor has completed a separate written contributor copyright-assignment agreement approved by the repository owner**.

The assignment process must identify the contribution and record the contributor's agreement in a form suitable for the applicable law. A pull request by itself is not treated here as a substitute for any signature or formal acceptance that the governing law may require.

## What the signed agreement is intended to cover

The contributor assignment used for accepted contributions is intended to transfer to the repository owner the contributor's copyright in the accepted contribution to the fullest extent permitted by law, including rights needed to:

- reproduce and modify the contribution;
- distribute it;
- incorporate it into this repository;
- relicense it;
- enforce rights in the contribution; and
- maintain the project under a centralized licensing model.

The contributor must retain responsibility for ensuring that the submitted material is their own work or that they otherwise have authority to assign the relevant rights.

## Third-party material is excluded

This requirement does not and cannot transfer copyright that a contributor does not own. Workshop-provided notebooks, PDFs, datasets, code, citations, and other third-party material remain subject to their original rights and terms.

## Current contribution procedure

Until an automated signing workflow is configured, contributors should open a pull request normally. The repository owner will not merge copyrightable contributions until the required written assignment has been completed.

## Legal review

Because copyright-assignment formalities vary by jurisdiction, the operative assignment form should be reviewed for the jurisdictions relevant to the repository owner and contributor before material outside contributions are accepted.
